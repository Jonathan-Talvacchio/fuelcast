      How this works

      Every day we pull the latest weekly average retail prices — regular, midgrade and premium gasoline, and diesel — from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) for your metro, state, or region, along with EIA's daily wholesale gasoline and diesel prices, crude oil, and its official monthly price outlook.

      The prediction is a simple, transparent estimate: recent momentum in your area's price, the direction wholesale gasoline has moved over the last couple of weeks (pump prices usually follow with a short lag), and — further out — EIA's monthly forecast for your region. The deal score asks two questions: how does today's price compare with the last 90 days in your area, and are prices expected to rise or fall over the next two weeks? A high score means "cheap now and likely to get pricier," so fill up.

      Tip: station prices often creep up ahead of the weekend; early-week fill-ups tend to be a little cheaper. Shop around too — the spread between the cheapest and priciest station in town is usually bigger than any day-to-day move.
